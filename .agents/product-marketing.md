# Product Marketing Context

**Document version:** v1
**Last updated:** 2026-10-28

## Product Overview
**One-liner:** Kairo dresses one man for four lives: the street, the boardroom, the old-money weekend and the open road.
**What it does:** An Ahmedabad menswear label with four complete wardrobes (Streetwear, Formal, Old Money, Riding) at one standard of quality, sold direct online with limited Drops.
**Product category:** Premium Indian menswear label, direct to customer
**Product type:** E-commerce (concept brand, portfolio showcase)
**Business model:** Accessible luxury: tees ~₹1,800, shirts/trousers ₹3,000–6,000, blazers and riding jackets ₹8,000–18,000

## Target Audience
**Who:** Men 18–30 in Indian cities; the Streetwear line is unisex
**Primary use case:** Look put-together in every setting with a few strong pieces a season
**Jobs to be done:**
- Find one label that covers street, office, weekends and riding
- Buy pieces that still look good after many washes
- Get something limited that friends don't have

## Problems & Pain Points
**Core problem:** Fast fashion looks cheap after three washes; global luxury is out of reach; no single label covers every mode at one standard.
**Emotional tension:** Wanting to look expensive without overspending; fear of the wrong fit when buying online.

## Competitive Landscape
**Direct:** Snitch, Rare Rabbit, Bombay Shirt Company, Bonkers Corner — each strong in one or two modes, not all four.
**Secondary:** Zara, H&M — trend-led, quality fades.
**Indirect:** The local tailor — great fit, slow, no streetwear or riding gear.

## Differentiation
- Four wardrobes under one label, designed to mix across collections
- Limited Drops instead of endless stock
- Ahmedabad textile roots as the quality story

## Objections
| Objection | Response |
|-----------|----------|
| Is the quality worth the price? | Fabric and construction notes on every product, close-up images, cost-per-wear framing |
| Will it fit me? | Size guide with body measurements, fit notes per product |
| What if I don't like it? | Clear returns and exchange policy near cart and checkout |
| Never heard of Kairo | About story, Verified-buyer reviews, lookbook |

**Anti-persona:** Shoppers hunting the lowest price or a ₹499 sale.

## Brand Voice
| Attribute | Means | Doesn't mean | Example line |
|-----------|-------|--------------|--------------|
| Confident | Plain statements, no begging | Arrogant or cold | "Cut once. Worn for years." |
| Short | Lines under 10 words | Cryptic | "Drop 04. Friday, 8 PM." |
| Cheeky | One wink per page | Meme overload, emoji | "Your old blazer called. It's tired." |
| Rooted | Proud of Ahmedabad and its cloth | Touristy clichés | "Woven in a city that's been doing this for 150 years." |

**Tone by context:**
- Product pages: facts first (fabric, fit, care), one line of attitude at most
- Drops: hype, countdowns, scarcity said once and plainly
- Errors and empty states: light and helpful
- Checkout and account: calm and exact, no jokes near money
- Contact: warm and human

**Collection voices:** Streetwear "Built for 2 AM." · Formal "Walk in. Close it." · Old Money "Never loud. Always noticed." · Riding "Made for the long way home."
**Terms we own:** fit, cut, drop, wardrobe, the four lives
**Never use (and why):** "premium quality", "best in class", "elevate your style", "curated", "unleash" — every store says them, so they read as filler.

## Proof Points
Concept brand: no real customers yet. Reviews seeded for the demo carry a visible "Sample" tag; only completed test orders earn "Verified buyer".

## Goals
**Business goal:** Show full-stack, premium e-commerce skill for Jainam's portfolio and agency
**Conversion action:** Shop a collection → add to bag → checkout. Secondary: contact the store
